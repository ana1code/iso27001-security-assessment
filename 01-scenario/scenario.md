# 🏢 Scenario Definition

## 1. Organization

CloudGest is a fictional SaaS company that provides an online business management and invoicing platform.

The platform is designed for organizations that need to manage commercial operations through a cloud-based environment.

## 2. Services

The platform provides several business functionalities, including:

* Invoicing and commercial documents
* Customer management
* Supplier management
* Sales and purchasing
* Stock management
* Web application access
* API integration
* Mobile applications

## 3. Information Assets

The platform processes and stores different types of business information, including:

* Customer information
* Supplier information
* Commercial documents
* Financial information
* User account information
* Authentication data
* Application and API data

## 4. Technology Environment

The service operates through a cloud-based infrastructure and relies on web applications, APIs, databases, mobile applications and third-party service providers.

This architecture provides scalability and accessibility, but also introduces additional cybersecurity risks.

## 5. Security Challenge

For this assessment, a security incident involving a critical cloud service provider is considered.

The incident may potentially affect the confidentiality, integrity or availability of CloudGest systems and information.

The organization must therefore identify relevant risks, evaluate their potential impact and implement appropriate security controls.

## 6. Assessment Scope

This assessment focuses on the information security controls and risk management practices that could be applied to the CloudGest environment using ISO 27001 and ISO 27002 principles.
